### Deceptive Design TPDL
During the summer of 2022, 3x3 and Superbloom consulted with 120 stakeholders as part of  a TPDL on moving [from deceptive design to trusted design](/files/deadlink.txt) <- deadlink to https://techlab.webfoundation.org/ddtd/overview. Combating deceptive design is a human rights issue that requires policy intervention to change tech company behavior and protect people from harm. The resulting launch of a multi-stakeholder coalition will continue the fight to normalize [recommendations for trusted design](https://airtable.com/app185ywjVuL0fFpr/shrzgr7Hv4J50zttl/tblaLmLopQFqBWULg) throughout industry.
