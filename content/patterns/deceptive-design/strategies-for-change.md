## Strategies for Change
The following strategies for Trusted Design were developed by a team of experts and advocates through the **Tech Policy Design Lab** [{{<pattern "Deceptive Design - Overview">}}] (TPDL). They focus on two areas: mobilising diverse changemakers and creating new industry standards.

### Mobilising for change
Through these strategies, diverse stakeholders across a range of contexts and industries will come together to change the conversation and advocate for Trusted Design.

![alt text][coalition-banner]
#### Build a Coalition to Mobilise Change
A coalition of diverse stakeholders initiating dialogue, promoting Trusted Design Norms, and supporting jurisdictional regulation at a global scale.
**Learn more.** [{{<pattern "Strategies - Coalition">}}]

![alt text][crowdsource-banner]
#### Create a Crowdsourced Reporting Tool
A reporting platform to make Deceptive Design more identifiable, reportable, and trackable for users and regulators.
**Learn more.** [{{<pattern "Strategies - Crowdsourced Reporting Tool">}}]

![alt text][storytelling-banner]
#### Launch a Storytelling Campaign
An inventory of global stories that explores people’s experiences with Deceptive and Trusted Design.
**Learn more.** [{{<pattern "Strategies - Storytelling Campaign">}}]

### Building new norms
Through these strategies, new norms and standards for Trusted Design will be created, guiding industry reform and regulation.

![alt text][trusted-banner]
#### Develop and Adopt Trusted Design Norms
A set of standards, principles, and guidelines that provide industry guidance on ethical UX/UI.
**Learn more.** [{{<pattern "Strategies - Trusted Design Norms">}}]

![alt text][evaluation-banner]
#### Develop Tools for Evaluation and Accreditation
A tool based on the Trusted Design Norms to assess the adherence to and impact of Trusted Design, as well as serve as the basis for regulations.
**Learn more.** [{{<pattern "Strategies - Develop Tools for Evaluation and Accreditation">}}]
